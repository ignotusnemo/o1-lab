Security analysis05 Aug 2026

Parano1d meets NIST PQC Category 1

Parano1d's production soundness profile is evaluated in the quantum random-oracle model against the NIST Category 1 resource reference for AES-128 key search. The proof begins with the exact wallet and HistoryStep relations, composes every represented root into one invalid-State event, converts typed parallel oracle access into logical gates and circuit depth, and includes finite extraction and collision terms. At the NIST gate-depth envelope, the complete ideal success bound is 0.053364140323608411. The dominant half-success floor is 173.273866314232 bits, 3.273866314232 bits above the 2^170 reference.

Protocol study15 Jul 2026

Why a block is accepted only with its recursive proof

An early design accepted a block first and produced its recursive proof later in the background. The canonical chain could therefore move ahead of the HistoryStep-proven tip, forcing nodes to persist and recover a second queue. The production design accepts the block and its matching HistoryStep together: one atomic object, one canonical height and no deferred proof backlog.
