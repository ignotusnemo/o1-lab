PaperJul 31, 2026

FROST-GKR: a global trace protocol for batched Poseidon2b

FROST-GKR represents every permutation slot, round and lane on one Boolean product domain. One degree-nine zero-check enforces the full round relation; one degree-two reduction returns shifted evaluations to the original committed columns. For the evaluated 59-permutation workload, the reduction cuts 472 per-permutation constraint sumchecks to two.
