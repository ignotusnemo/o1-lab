The calculation retains twenty historical events and adds six current events. Binary-field proximity and grouped restoration

Counterexamples to Beyond-Johnson Proximity Gaps over Binary Fields, first received on 9 October, makes the radius and characteristic conditions decisive. The inspected PDF is its November revision. The production check evaluates the exact finite Johnson margin on every selected layer.

Authorization uses agreement 1/5 and rate 1/32. Every checked authorization layer satisfies (1/5)² > (K−1)/N. History and retained sparse openings use rate 1/4 and their selected agreement above 1/2. Their finite margins are also positive. The receipt covers all 421 current and retained layers.

Mutual Correlated Agreement and List-Decoding: Improvements and Separations, received on 23 October and revised on 3 November, gives a finite-list correction to a generic transfer threshold. At the authorization parameters, with list radius and requested MCA radius 4/5 and list envelope 17, its positive-gap expression is negative. This identifies the exact need for an RS-specific common-agreement argument.

The 7 November grouped-restoration proof supplies that argument using Theorem 4.6 of Ben-Sasson, Carmon, Haböck, Kopparty and Saraf, On Proximity Gaps for Reed–Solomon Codes. It checks the strict Johnson and multiplicity hypotheses on all 421 authenticated layers. The theorem's simultaneous agreement-set quantifier permits the candidate and its agreement set to be selected after the challenge.

Weighted backward restoration then reconnects row batching, consecutive binary folds and committed middle words to their source. The retained previous stage supplies the weight indicator. Inverse butterflies preserve the required weighted agreement mass. The exact receipt records each finite domain and multiplicity. This establishes the public-coin code-level restoration result. Its native adaptive recursive use enters the coupled all-root condition.

The resource theorem and two global conditions

The resource-closure theorem proves the complete charged ideal envelope. Let κj be the source-linked bad-response density and cj its declared response price. With ρ = maxj(κj/cj), each permitted batch obeys its gate-depth charge, and the complete schedule obeys the total gate and depth budgets G and D.

(Σs √(10 Σj κj ksj))² ≤ 10ρGD.

Cauchy–Schwarz gives the displayed bound. The complete envelope E(G,D) also includes the typed finite and global collision terms. The production lifting defines Δfixed(G,D) over all allowed adversaries within the actual total logical resource budget, including permitted cheaper circuits, batch sharing, adversarial preprocessing and verification queries.

Prfixed[BadState] ≤ E(G,D) + Δfixed(G,D).

The current production statement has two global conditions:

  1. Coupled all-root composition. Accepted native and arithmetized roots, recognizers, local extraction and retained ancestry are related in one adaptive shared-oracle experiment.
  2. Resource-aware fixed-Poseidon2b deviation. The compiler-event deviation is bounded over the actual permitted gate and depth budgets, with the published setup and advice policy.

At the Category 1 reference points, the complete ideal envelope is at most approximately 0.0493738837. A fixed-event deviation below the exact reported headroom, approximately 0.4506261163, places the resulting success bound below one half. Each MAXDEPTH point retains its own resource budget. The source and resource arguments preserve the numerical certificate while making these production conditions explicit.
