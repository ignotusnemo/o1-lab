About O(1) Lab

Research that survives implementation.

O(1) Lab is the protocol research group behind ParanO(1)d. The journal preserves both the finished work and the experiments that changed the design.

01 · Scope

What belongs here

This journal covers proof systems, arithmetization, polynomial commitments, recursive verification, binary-field implementation and the protocol consequences of those choices. It is not the user documentation for ParanO(1)d. Operational instructions live in the project documentation; this site records the reasoning and evidence behind the machinery.

02 · Record

Positive and negative results

A failed implementation can be more useful than a clean final diagram. When an approach ran out of memory, missed the block cadence or exposed a binding gap, the article states the failure before the replacement. Removed prototypes are described only when a preserved commit, benchmark or test still supports the account.

03 · Evidence

Numbers keep their conditions

Performance figures identify the workload and machine that produced them. Proof-size figures state whether they include serialization, a polynomial-commitment opening and Merkle authentication. A preserved benchmark is not presented as the current production path, and an implemented milestone is not presented as a complete protocol if later work was still required.

04 · Project

Relationship to ParanO(1)d

O(1) Lab is the research group behind ParanO(1)d, a proof-native L1 Statechain secured by proof of work. The codebase is where most of this work was tested under production constraints: a fifteen-second block target, bounded state, signatureless ownership and a recursive HistoryStep that authenticates the present without replaying transaction history.
